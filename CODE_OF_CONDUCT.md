# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Agent Project Kit to be welcoming and constructive for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give clear, constructive feedback focused on the work.
- Welcome questions and different perspectives.
- Accept responsibility and correct mistakes when they happen.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and sharing someone's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this code and may restrict participation for repeated or serious violations. To report a concern, contact a project maintainer privately through GitHub.
